## Part 6 — Verdict

**M10.0a_READY_FOR_OWNER_APPROVAL → APPROVED.** All corrections ratified, all open decisions
resolved. The most consequential outcome: **C-3 is answered by an existing ratified pattern rather
than new architecture** — ADR-0031's IR1a (scoped sub-package exception, per-layer assertions
tightened rather than relaxed, plane brings its own `Database`) is exactly the shape Epic 10 needs
and has already been through a full certification cycle. ADR-0034 therefore restates an established
precedent for a second plane rather than making a novel structural decision.
