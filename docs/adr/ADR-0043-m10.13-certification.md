## Status

**Accepted — 2026-08-21.** Every ruling below was issued by the Technical Owner at the M10.13
architectural decision gate, in response to a read-only pre-flight that found **two of §M10.13's
thirteen named minimum controls factually false against the repository as it stands** — both because
of changes the Owner had previously authorized — and **one mandatory action already discharged**. The
document was drafted as `Proposed`, reviewed at the Proposed stage, and ratified with three further
rulings confirmed at that review: the thirteen-control decomposition stands (**CD1**), the Epic 10
certification module is **independent** and lives beside Epic 9's without touching it (**CD2**), and
**no decision-log entry** accompanies this ratification (**CD11**). The rulings below are a record of a
decision made, not a proposal.

**Ratification authorizes the M10.13 architecture only.** It does not authorize certification
implementation, certification tests, production changes, migration changes, billing changes,
modification of Epic 9 certification artifacts, M10.14/M10.15 work, staging, commit, push or tag.

Unlike ADR-0042, whose milestone text was two lines, **§M10.13 is well specified**. This ADR therefore
does far less inventing: it restates thirteen already-named controls as executable verification,
resolves exactly three collisions between that text and the authorized repository state, and draws the
boundary.

## Context

### §M10.13 in full

> ### M10.13 — Certification · **mandatory DL-0139 revisit**
>
> Epic 10 defines its **own** control set with fresh identifiers; it does not renumber, reuse or
> extend Epic 9's 27, and does not re-run or reinterpret them. Minimum controls: `billing/**`
> unchanged; migrations `0001`–`0033` unchanged and Epic 10 `≥ 0034`; `evt_*` prefix conformance;
> existing `EventType` members unchanged; no `metric.billing.*` defined by Epic 10; Billing holds no
> `EventBus` subscription; no pre-existing `api/` file modified; `platform/replay/**` unchanged;
> `api/**/observability*` unchanged; Epic 9 digests reproduced verbatim as historical state; `NS-5`,
> `ST-3` and the nine limitations still open with their owners. Preserve the digest/verdict
> separation. LIM-7 (self-administered certification) is inherited honestly.
>
> **Mandatory: DL-0139 must be revisited here.** The three structural release-suite assertions must be
> resolved by a **separate Owner-authorized governance decision**. Note that merely adding an Epic 10
> release-certification suite carrying the generalized invariant does **not** resolve the existing
> Epic 9 assertions, which would continue to fail.

### Epic 9 supplies the form, and is not to be touched

`tests/certification/catalogue.py` holds `CERTIFICATION_CONTROLS` — exactly **27** controls across the
families `DT`, `NS`, `OWN`, `PL`, `PY`, `RS`, `ST`, `TX` — plus `DECLARED_LIMITATIONS` (nine, `LIM-1`
… `LIM-9`, `LIM-7` owned by `M9.24`), `CERTIFICATION_VERSION = "m9.24.1"`, a three-value
`ControlOutcome` in which `INCONCLUSIVE` is deliberately **not** a passing outcome, a three-value
`CertificationVerdict`, and `certification_digest()` — a content digest over *what was found*, computed
independently of the verdict. That last separation is the "digest/verdict separation" §M10.13 requires
be preserved: **the digest records evidence; the verdict interprets it; neither is derived from the
other.** Epic 10 inherits the *shape* and touches none of the *substance*.

### Ten of thirteen controls already hold; three collide

Verified read-only against `v4.0.0` (`532b0bd7`) at HEAD `8ee7d3c1`:

| Named control | State |
|---|---|
| migrations `0001`–`0033` unchanged; Epic 10 `≥ 0034` | holds — 0 changes; Epic 10 owns `0034/0035/0036` |
| `evt_*` prefix conformance | holds — three tables, all indexes `ix_evt_*` |
| existing `EventType` members unchanged | holds — additive only (`METRIC_TURN`) |
| no `metric.billing.*` defined by Epic 10 | holds — appears only in a namespace-reservation comment |
| Billing holds no `EventBus` subscription | holds |
| no pre-existing `api/` file modified | holds — **additions only** (`api/metering/**`) |
| `platform/replay/**` unchanged | holds — 0 changes |
| `api/**observability*` unchanged | holds — 0 changes |
| `NS-5`, `ST-3` and the nine limitations open with owners | holds |
| digest/verdict separation | holds |
| **`billing/**` unchanged** | **false** — `platform/billing/foundation/catalog.py` is `+8/-0` |
| **Epic 9 digests reproduced verbatim as historical state** | **false** — replaced at M10.10 |
| **DL-0139 revisited here** | **already discharged at M10.10** |

The first collision is the ADR-0039 Amendment 1 **MB14** member `MeterSource.OBSERVABILITY =
"observability"`, added under a one-member additive Owner exception. The second is the M10.10
re-baseline of the Epic 9 release record: manifest digest `eed69222…` → `cdafb4ab…`, certification
digest `39790b95…` → `c044b3f6…`. The third is DL-0139's own resolution text, which states that **"the
planned M10.13 revisit is no longer required for it."**

All three changes were Owner-authorized. **A certification control that fails on authorized state is a
defective control, not a discovered defect** — and equally, deleting the control to make the suite
green would be certifying by omission. The rulings below take the third path: state the exception
exactly, and prove the original values still exist.

## Decision

### CD1 — Fresh Epic 10 identifier namespace

Epic 10's controls use the prefix **`E10-`**, numbered `E10-1` … `E10-13`. The namespace collides with
no Epic 9 family (`DT`, `NS`, `OWN`, `PL`, `PY`, `RS`, `ST`, `TX`), no ADR ruling prefix, and no
declared limitation. Epic 10 declares its own version constant, distinct from `m9.24.1`.

### CD2 — Epic 9's 27 controls are not renumbered, reused, extended or modified, and Epic 10's module is independent

`tests/certification/catalogue.py` and `tests/certification/runner.py` are **not edited**.
`CERTIFICATION_CONTROLS` remains 27 entries. `DECLARED_LIMITATIONS` remains nine.
`CERTIFICATION_VERSION` remains `"m9.24.1"`.

Epic 10's control set is an **independent module and suite** placed **under the existing
`tests/certification/` area**. The exact module path is the implementation's choice; the **independence
boundary is not**. Co-location is filesystem adjacency only and confers no coupling:

* Epic 10 declares its **own** controls, its **own** limitations and its **own** version constant.
* Epic 10 **never** adds to, renumbers, reuses, wraps or re-exports `CERTIFICATION_CONTROLS`,
  `CONTROLS_BY_ID`, `DECLARED_LIMITATIONS` or `CERTIFICATION_VERSION`.
* Epic 9's catalogue is imported — if at all — **read-only, as evidence about Epic 9**, never as a
  source of Epic 10 controls and never to execute one (**CD3**).
* Epic 9's `runner.py` control implementations are **never invoked** by Epic 10.

Shared *shape* (the outcome vocabulary, the verdict vocabulary, the digest discipline) is inherited by
**restatement in Epic 10's own vocabulary**, not by mutating Epic 9's definitions.

### CD3 — Epic 9's 27 controls are not executed as M10.13 certification controls

M10.13 does not run, re-run, wrap, re-derive or reinterpret them. Epic 9's certification stands as the
historical record it already is. Whatever verdict Epic 9 reached, it reached in M9.24; M10.13 neither
confirms nor disturbs it.

### CD4 — The minimum control set is executable verification, not prose

Each of `E10-1` … `E10-13` is a control that **runs** and yields an outcome. A control that cannot
gather its evidence is `INCONCLUSIVE`, and `INCONCLUSIVE` does **not** pass — the Epic 9 rule at
`catalogue.py` §`PASSING_OUTCOMES`, inherited unchanged.

### CD5 — The `billing/**` control carries exactly one exception, and it is enumerated

**`E10-1`** certifies `platform/billing/**` unchanged since `v4.0.0` **except** the single additive
member `MeterSource.OBSERVABILITY = "observability"` in `platform/billing/foundation/catalog.py`
(ADR-0039 Amendment 1, **MB14**, M10.10). The exception is expressed as a **closed, exact allow-list**
in the shape of the existing `PERMITTED_BILLING_EDGES` precedent — naming the file, the symbol and the
authorizing decision — never as a path glob, a file-level waiver or a diff-size tolerance.

Any other `billing/**` change fails `E10-1`: a second added member, a modified member, a deleted
member, a modified file, a new file, a removed file. **The exception is historical and one-member. It
does not grow.** ADR-0039 is neither reopened nor amended, and the billing boundary is unchanged.

### CD6 — Epic 9's historical digests are proven, not restored

**`E10-10`** certifies that the **original** Epic 9 digest values —
`eed692224f0aeb82c1a64e8edb39b3b553a709c67cf5d37ae50090bcb466b65c` (manifest) and
`39790b95d061a7631e6d1b61dab14b227f02038873ee87b5c6a34658bb01d2d8` (certification) — remain
**reproducible as historical evidence** from the `v4.0.0` baseline or another already-authorized
historical source.

M10.13 **MUST NOT** revert, edit or recompute the current post-M10.10 release, freeze or certification
artifacts in order to recreate them. The control certifies a **two-state distinction** and asserts both
halves:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Original Epic 9 certified state** | the historical digests, as of `v4.0.0` |
| **Current repository state** | the authorized post-M10.10 values |

Neither is presented as the other. M10.10 is not reinterpreted or reopened; ADR-0039 is not altered;
Epic 9's 27 controls are not changed.

### CD7 — "No pre-existing `api/` file modified" means modification, not addition

**`E10-7`** certifies that no file existing under `platform/api/**` at `v4.0.0` has been **modified or
deleted**. Files **added** by Epic 10 under `api/**` (`api/metering/**`) are permitted and are not
violations — §M10.13 says *"no pre-existing `api/` file modified"*, and a file that did not exist
cannot be pre-existing. The control asserts this distinction explicitly rather than leaving it to the
reader.

### CD8 — Digest/verdict separation is preserved structurally

Epic 10's report carries a content digest over **what was found** and a verdict over **what it means**,
computed independently, with neither derived from the other, following `certification_digest()`. A
control's outcome never feeds the digest input as a verdict, and the verdict never enters the digest
payload.

### CD9 — `NS-5`, `ST-3` and the nine limitations remain open, with their existing owners

**`E10-11`** certifies that the two Epic 9 advisories and all nine `LIM-` limitations are still
declared, still open, and still carry the **owners they already have**. M10.13 closes none of them,
retargets none of them, and reassigns no ownership.

### CD10 — LIM-7 is inherited honestly

Epic 10's certification is **self-administered**, exactly as Epic 9's was. This is declared in the
report as an inherited limitation, in Epic 10's own vocabulary, without weakening or re-owning Epic 9's
`LIM-7` (owner `M9.24`). An Epic 10 certification is a **structural self-assessment**, and says so.

### CD11 — DL-0139 is recorded as discharged, not revisited

§M10.13's mandatory revisit is **satisfied**: DL-0139 was discharged at **M10.10** by Owner-authorized
retarget plus commit `3ca738e5`, and its own resolution states *"M10.13 revisit no longer required."*
The decision log is Level-3 governance and the implementation plan is a plan; the higher and newer
authority controls — the same supersession pattern ADR-0041 **RT5** recorded for DL-0141 against plan
§M10.5.

M10.13 **records this disposition** as certification/governance evidence. It does **not** reopen,
modify, reinterpret, extend or create a new substantive decision for DL-0139. **No decision-log
modification is authorized by this ADR.** The §M10.13 sentence is stale text, in the same way its
completion markers were stale before the roadmap-marker correction.

### CD12 — Certification reads; it does not write

Every control is a **read-only structural assertion**. M10.13 introduces no production module, no
repository, no table, no command bus, no port method and no runtime behaviour — the explicit Epic 9
certification precedent (*"no table, no migration, no repository and no command bus"*), inherited.

### CD13 — No migration

The chain remains at `0034/0035/0036`. Certification observes the migration chain; it does not extend
it.

### CD14 — No production wiring

Nothing constructs, registers, schedules or exposes an Epic 10 certification surface in production.
There is no endpoint, no ops API, no scheduler and no background task.

### CD15 — No Accepted ADR is modified

ADR-0023, ADR-0031, ADR-0032 – ADR-0042 remain immutable and intact. **ED1–ED12**, **DS1–DS13**,
**DW1–DW11**, **RL1–RL17**, **RC1–RC16**, **MS1–MS10**, **TP1–TP11**, **MB1–MB16**, **SV1–SV12**,
**RT1–RT14**, **HD1–HD16**, **AR-31**, **AR-32** and **AR-42** all continue to bind.

### CD16 — No existing test is weakened

Epic 9's certification, freeze and release suites are **not modified**. The three structurally-false
Epic 9 release assertions recorded in DL-0139 keep their current disposition; M10.13 neither repairs
nor re-breaks them, and adding an Epic 10 suite does **not** resolve them — §M10.13 says so directly.
Every M10.13 guard is additive.

### CD17 — Out of scope

**M10.14**, **M10.15**, **Epic 11** and **Epic 12** are out of scope, and M10.13 asserts nothing about
them. Also out of scope: the v5.0.0 freeze and release, any operator control surface, any real-provider
behaviour, any production latency claim, and any renumbering of Epic 9.

## Rejected interpretations

|---|---|---|
| **CR1** | **Extend Epic 9's `CERTIFICATION_CONTROLS` to 27 + n** | §M10.13 forbids renumbering, reusing and extending. It would also mutate a released, digested Epic 9 artifact and drift its digest. |
| **CR2** | **Re-run Epic 9's 27 controls as Epic 10's evidence** | §M10.13 forbids re-running and reinterpreting. Epic 9's verdict is historical; re-deriving it now would silently re-certify a frozen epic against a moved repository. |

## Consequences

**Gained.** Epic 10 acquires its own certification with its own vocabulary, independent of a frozen
epic's · two controls that would have failed on authorized state are made truthful by enumeration
rather than by omission · the historical Epic 9 digests stop being invisible, becoming provable
evidence instead of a value that used to be in a file · the stale §M10.13 DL-0139 mandate is resolved
on the record rather than by silence · Epic 9's released artifacts stay untouched.

**Accepted cost.** The `billing/**` control carries a permanent enumerated exception, which is a
standing reminder that Epic 10 touched a frozen plane once — that visibility is the point. Proving
historical digests requires reading git history from a test, which couples one control to repository
history; this is the same coupling DL-0139 recorded as debt for Epic 9's release suite, here confined
to a single control that reads a **tag**, not `HEAD`, and so cannot become structurally false as
development continues.

## Known limitations

Recorded plainly rather than implied by silence:

* **Epic 10's certification is self-administered** — LIM-7, inherited honestly. No independent party
  verifies it.
* **The controls are structural, not behavioural.** They certify that boundaries, prefixes, vocabularies
  and artifacts are what they claim to be. They do not certify that the durable spine behaves correctly
  under load, in production, or against real providers.
* **`E10-10` depends on the `v4.0.0` tag remaining reachable.** In a clone without tags or history, the
  control is `INCONCLUSIVE` — which does not pass, by design.
* **M10.13 does not resolve the three Epic 9 release assertions** recorded in DL-0139; §M10.13 states so
  itself, and they require their own Owner-authorized governance decision.
* **M10.13 certifies; it does not freeze or release.** v5.0.0 remains M10.14/M10.15 work.
* **No production latency claim.** I1 remains the open Epic 11 obligation ADR-0042 HD3 recorded.

## Non-goals

Renumbering, reusing, extending, modifying or re-running Epic 9's 27 controls · modifying Epic 9's
certification, freeze or release artifacts · modifying `billing/**` · modifying migrations · reopening
DL-0139 or DL-0141 · modifying `.voxvoice/DECISION_LOG.md` · amending ADR-0039 or any other Accepted
ADR · reinterpreting or reopening M10.10 · reverting post-M10.10 artifacts · production wiring ·
operator control · a read API · the v5.0.0 freeze or release · M10.14 · M10.15 · Epic 11 · Epic 12 ·
unrelated refactoring.
